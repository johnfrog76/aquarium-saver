Don't take our word for it

The tanks make a few promises — no clock under seven seconds, salt water and the planted garden never in one tank, one light each, and reduced motion showing the resting pose rather than frame zero. Press Run and they are checked in front of you, now, against the pages this site is actually serving.

Not a badge and not a recording. The button fetches index.html, atoms.html and counterparts.html and hands them to tools/invariants.mjs — the same module npm test runs under node:test, and the same one CI gates on. If a tank breaks and ships anyway, this page goes red on its own.

The checks get their own room because the three tank pages carry no script at all, and that is the point of them: nothing runs while you are watching a tank. npm test is still the authoritative run.
